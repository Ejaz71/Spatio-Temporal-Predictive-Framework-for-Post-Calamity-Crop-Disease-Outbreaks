Real NASA POWER meteorology, real Sentinel-1 RTC calibrated SAR backscatter, and real Landsat NDVI/NDWI/land-surface-temperature — fetched per event, not fabricated.

MODELS

Classical Baselines vs. Fusion Model

Random Forest and XGBoost, evaluated with grouped-by-district and leave-one-event-out cross-validation, compared fairly against a CNN-LSTM-style fusion model.

DECISION

Honest Stage 3/4 Outcome

The fusion model did not clear the classical baseline by a statistically defensible margin on n=77 — RandomForest is reported as the primary result.
